02 · Our approach

Good technique should give you more freedom.

01

A natural starting point

02

Technique you can feel

Breath, vowels, resonance and colour are taught in practical steps. You will understand what is changing and why it works.

03 · Technique

The foundations we return to.

We have learned from teachers and coaches around the world. Methods differ, but the foundations and destination are remarkably consistent.

01 Breath

We train the natural relationship between breath and the lower abdominals, then add simple resistance exercises and sliding scales. It is an effective warm-up for beginners and experienced singers alike.

02 Vowels

Pure vowels make singing freer and clearer. We work through ee, eh, ah, oh and oo, moving from one vowel to the next with less effort and better consistency across pop, jazz, musical theatre and classical repertoire.

03 Colour

Lessons give you options and a useful language for sound. Brightness, twang, warmth and sob quality can all serve a lyric. The emotion in a song often reveals colours you did not know you had.

04 Recording

Our studio has recording equipment available for every vocal stream. Listening back can help you hear progress, make adjustments and prepare a quick demo when needed.
